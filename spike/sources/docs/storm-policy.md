# Severe weather operations policy

The formal ops-side policy behind the storm pause, covering both what the system does automatically and what drivers are expected to do when it kicks in. The wiki page covers the same rule more casually; this is the version to point to if a driver or a new ops hire needs the official wording.

## Purpose

Fleet operations shouldn't continue as normal once conditions get bad enough to put a driver or a rider at meaningful risk. This policy defines exactly where that line sits and what happens automatically once it's crossed, so nobody has to make that call ad hoc in the moment.

## Pause

Rebalancing pauses automatically once the latest Skyglass severity reading reaches 3 or higher for a station. No dispatcher decision is required — the system enforces this on its own the moment a reading crosses that line, and resumes on its own once readings drop back under it.

## Vans

Drivers on an active route when a pause triggers return to the depot rather than continuing or waiting at their current stop. They stay at the depot until the pause lifts before heading back out — this is a firm instruction, not a judgment call left to the driver in the moment.

## What isn't covered here

This policy only governs rebalancing. It has no bearing on the maintenance sweep, which keeps running through a storm exactly as it would on a clear day — a bike that needs to come off the road for fault reports still gets locked regardless of conditions outside.

## Communication during a pause

Ops should post in the storm channel the moment a pause triggers, even though the system handles the pause itself automatically — drivers and support both need a heads-up that vans are heading back rather than finding out from a dispatcher call mid-shift.
