# Spring promotion retrospective

Notes from the retro on how the spring free-minutes promotion went, now that it's wrapped up. Circulated to product and fares; sharing more broadly since a few people outside those teams asked about it.

**Attendees:** Dana Whitlock, Mara Voss, Kasia Brandt, Joel Ferraz

## Dates

The promotion ran from 2026-02-20 through 2026-06-10. That's the window we're evaluating in this retro — any ridership or support-ticket numbers referenced in the discussion below are scoped to that period specifically, not to the rate going forward.

## What went well

Ridership among members climbed noticeably during the window, and support didn't see a spike in confused tickets about the rate — the messaging in the app made the free-minutes change clear enough that most riders didn't need to ask. Framing it as a limited-time promotion rather than a quiet pricing change also made it easy to message clearly in the app and in support macros.

## What was rocky

A few support tickets came in from riders assuming the promotion would revert entirely rather than settle into an ongoing rate, since "promotion" implies something temporary. Worth being more precise with that word next time we do something similar, so expectations are set correctly from the start.

## Decision

Given the ridership data, product decided to keep the rate in place going forward rather than reverting — see Spring promotion 2026 on the wiki for the rate itself and how it's described now that it's permanent rather than a promotion.

## Action items

- Dana: update external messaging to stop referring to the rate as a "promotion" going forward.
- Joel: update the support macro that still references the old promotional framing.
- Mara: confirm the rate is reflected correctly on the next billing cycle's invoices.
- Kasia: check whether any other flag-driven pricing pages use similarly temporary-sounding language that could cause the same confusion later.

## Format note

Going forward, product would like promotion retros like this one to include a short "what we'd do differently" section rather than folding it into the general discussion — noting that here so it's not missed on the next one.
