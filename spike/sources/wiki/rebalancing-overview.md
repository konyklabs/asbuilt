# Rebalancing

The logic behind why bikes get moved overnight, for anyone who's ever wondered why a van shows up at a station that looks perfectly fine.

## Trigger

A station gets flagged for a rebalance order once it drops under 25% full. Below that, there just aren't enough bikes left for riders showing up first thing, so it goes on the list for that night's run.

## Target

When a rebalance order goes out, it's not a token top-up — enough bikes move to bring that station all the way up to 50% full. That gives it real headroom in both directions: enough bikes for a morning rush, enough empty docks for the evening one.

## Vans

Van shifts start at 06:00, ahead of the morning commute so stations are sorted before riders show up looking for a bike. Drivers get their route sheet the night before so there's no scrambling at the depot in the morning.
