# Spring promotion 2026

Write-up of the free-minutes change we shipped this spring, for anyone landing here from an old link or a support ticket that references "the promotion."

## Free Minutes

The headline change: every member ride now starts with 30 minutes free before per-minute billing kicks in, on every ride, no cap on how many rides a day get it. That's the number that's live for members today — if you see an older figure referenced anywhere, this page is the current one.

## Dates

The promotion ran from 2026-02-20 through 2026-06-10. That window is mostly historical context at this point rather than something support needs to act on day to day.

## Why we ran it as a promotion first

Product wanted usage data before committing to the rate permanently, so it launched framed as a limited-time offer rather than a quiet pricing change. That let us watch ridership without locking ourselves into anything before we'd seen how riders actually responded.
