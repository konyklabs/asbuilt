# Van operations

Day-to-day logistics for the rebalancing fleet — not the rebalancing logic itself, just what drivers need to know to show up and do the job.

## Shift

Drivers clock on at 05:30 and head straight to the depot to pick up that night's route sheet before heading out. Anyone showing up later than that is already behind the first stop on their list, since the route is built around that start time.

## Depot logistics

Vans are assigned the night before, not the morning of — check the board at the depot rather than assuming you'll get the same van as last time. Keys live in the lockbox by the depot office; if a van's key isn't there, it's probably already out or in for service, check with whoever's on the desk before assuming it's missing.

## Vehicle checks

Every driver does a quick walk-around before pulling out: tires, lights, the ramp latch. It takes a couple of minutes and it's saved us from more than one van going out with a problem that should've been caught at the depot instead of on a route.

## Who to call

Radio or call dispatch directly if a route can't be finished for any reason — a breakdown, a blocked station, anything. Don't just skip a stop and hope someone notices; flag it so the stop gets picked up on the next run.
