# E-bike surcharge

The extra charge for riding electric, and how it plays with the cap that everyone always asks about.

## Rule

With ebike_surcharge on, every minute of an e-bike ride that actually gets billed picks up an extra $0.10 on top of the normal per-minute rate. It only touches billed minutes — a member's free window still doesn't get charged anything at all, surcharge included, since there's nothing to add ten cents to.

## Cap

The single-ride cap doesn't carve out an exception for the surcharge — it applies to the whole ride total, surcharge and all. So a long e-bike ride that would've hit the cap anyway on the base rate alone just hits it a little sooner with the surcharge stacked on; the rider never ends up paying more than the cap either way.

## A common mix-up

Support sometimes assumes the surcharge is a flat fee like the unlock charge — it isn't, it's per minute, same as the base rate. Worth double-checking which one a rider is asking about before answering.
