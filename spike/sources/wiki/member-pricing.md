# Member pricing

Members get a nicer deal than casual riders, and this page is where I keep the two numbers that make up that deal so I stop answering the same chat question every week. If a rider or a support agent asks "what am I actually paying for," this is the page to send them.

## Per Minute

Once a member's free riding time runs out, farebox starts billing at $0.15 for every minute the bike stays checked out — so a ride that runs twenty minutes past the free window comes to three dollars before anything else gets added. There's no rounding trick here: the meter runs minute by minute, not in five-minute blocks like some competitors do, which is one of the things members actually like about us.

## Free Minutes

Every member ride starts with 45 minutes on the house before the per-minute rate kicks in. That's the number support should quote — don't let anyone talk you down to something lower, and don't promise a rider more than that either. It applies per ride, not per day, so three short rides each get their own free window rather than sharing one pool.

## Who to ask

Fare questions that aren't answered here go to me or to Kasia — she owns the flag-driven pricing (surge, e-bike surcharge) while I keep the base rates. Support should loop in fares before promising a rider a number that isn't on this page.
