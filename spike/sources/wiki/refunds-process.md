# Refunds process

The rules support needs before promising a rider money back. Read this before you tell anyone "sure, you'll get a refund" — some requests just can't be honored no matter how reasonable they sound.

## Window

A refund request has to come in within 14 days of the ride ending. Past that, farebox rejects it outright and there's no manual override support can reach for — if a rider waited longer than that to complain, the answer is no, however unfair that feels on a case-by-case basis.

## Limits

A refund can never be for more than the ride actually cost. That sounds obvious, but it comes up more than you'd think when a rider asks for "a refund plus something for the trouble" — the something-extra part isn't a refund, it's a goodwill gesture, and those go through a different process entirely.

## Review

Every refund request gets looked at by a fares agent before it's paid out. Don't tell a rider their money is back the moment you submit the request — it goes into the fares queue and sits there until someone signs off on it, however small the amount.

## Common support questions

"Can I get a refund for a ride from last month?" — no, it's past the window, say so plainly. "Can I get extra for the inconvenience?" — that's not a refund, escalate separately.
