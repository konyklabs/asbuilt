# On-call handbook

General notes on being on-call across the three teams, from the platform side. Team-specific escalation paths live on each team's own alert pages.

## Handover

The rotation changes hands every Monday at 09:00. Whoever's coming off should leave notes on anything still open — a flaky alert, an incident that isn't fully closed out, anything the next person shouldn't be surprised by.

## What to have ready before your shift starts

Make sure you can actually get paged before your shift begins, not after the first page doesn't reach you. Test your access to the dashboards and the deploy tooling too — Monday morning is a bad time to discover you're locked out of something.

## During your shift

Acknowledge a page quickly even if you haven't diagnosed anything yet — a quick "looking into it" beats silence for whoever's watching the incident channel. If you're stuck, escalate rather than sitting on it; nobody's going to fault you for asking sooner.

## Related pages

Farebox alerts and Dispatch alerts have the specific triggers you might get paged for. Deploying Gearwell has the deploy windows worth knowing before your shift.
