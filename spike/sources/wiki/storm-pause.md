# Storm pause

New rule as of this quarter: rebalancing now backs off on its own when the weather turns bad enough. This page is the whole thing — rule, scope, and what it means for drivers.

## Rule

The threshold is a Skyglass severity reading of 3 or higher at the nearest station. While the latest reading sits at or above that level, rebalancing simply doesn't run — no orders go out, no vans get dispatched, until the reading drops back under it.

## Scope

This only touches rebalancing. The maintenance sweep doesn't care about the weather at all and keeps running exactly as normal through a storm — a bike that needs to come off the road for fault reports still gets locked whether or not it's pouring outside.

## Vans

When a pause kicks in, van drivers don't sit around wherever they happen to be — they head back to the depot and wait there until the pause lifts. That's as much a safety call as an operational one; nobody wants a driver mid-route deciding for themselves whether conditions are still fine.

## Questions to send my way

If a van hasn't come back to the depot within a reasonable time of a pause starting, that's worth a call to whoever's driving it directly, not a wiki lookup.
