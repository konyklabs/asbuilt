# Invoicing

How members and casual riders actually get billed — two very different paths that land in the same place: a charge on a card.

## Monthly

Members don't get charged per ride. Instead, farebox builds one invoice per calendar month for each member, with every ride from that month showing up as its own line on it. So a member who rode a dozen times in a month sees a dozen lines, not one lump sum, which makes it a lot easier to spot a ride that looks wrong.

## Casual

Casual riders skip the invoice entirely — the moment their ride closes, farebox charges their card through Tollbooth Pay right then, no batching, no waiting for a monthly cycle. That's the fastest path from "ride ends" to "card charged" in the whole system.

## Why the difference

Members ride often enough that a monthly statement makes sense and saves them a card charge every single trip; casual riders don't have a standing relationship with us, so charging immediately is both simpler and safer.
