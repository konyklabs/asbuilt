# Fares overview

A short summary of the pricing rules that sit outside the base per-minute rates — the extras and the ceiling. For the base member and casual rates, see the pricing pages linked below; this one covers what changes on top of them.

## Ebike

When the ebike_surcharge flag is on, every billable minute of a ride on an e-bike picks up an extra $0.10 on top of whatever the base per-minute rate already charges. It only applies to minutes that are actually charged, so it stacks on top of a member's per-minute rate the same way it does for a casual rider, and it never applies during a member's free window since nothing is being charged there anyway.

## Cap

A single ride is capped at $25.00 regardless of how long the bike stays out or how many extras land on top of the per-minute rate. Farebox stops adding to the total once it hits that number; the rider is never charged more than that for one ride, full stop.
