# Membership FAQ

Answers to the questions support gets asked most about signing up. If a rider emails asking "how much" or "what happens if my card fails," point them here before typing out a fresh answer.

## Plans

There are two ways to become a member: pay $15.00 every month, or pay $120.00 once a year. Riders sometimes ask which one is "better" — that's really a question about how often they ride, not something support should have an opinion on, but the annual option is the one we quietly nudge people toward if they ask.

## Grace

Cards fail all the time — expired, over the limit, bank flagged it as fraud. When that happens, a member doesn't lose their membership right away: they get a 3-day grace period to fix their payment method before anything lapses. During that window they're still treated as a member for pricing, so don't tell a rider their rate has changed just because a renewal bounced.

## If a rider asks for a refund on a plan

That's not a fares FAQ question — send it straight to Mara or Kasia. This page only covers what the plans cost and what happens when a card fails.
