# Support playbook

The scripts I actually use when a ticket comes in about pricing or a lost bike. If you're new to support, bookmark this one — it comes up constantly.

## Free Minutes

When a rider asks why they got charged on what they thought was a "free" ride, the answer is almost always that they went past their free window. Members get 45 minutes free on every ride before billing starts — quote that number confidently, it's not something that varies by plan or by rider.

## Lost Bike Waiver

If a rider's account got hit with a lost-bike fee but the bike actually turns up, support has some room to work with: the fee can be waived as long as the bike is found within 72 hours of the ride closing. Past that window, don't offer a waiver yourself — escalate to fares instead.

## Tone reminders

Nobody wants to hear "that's just the policy" — explain the why in plain terms even when the answer is still no. Most riders accept a clear explanation even when it's not the answer they wanted.
