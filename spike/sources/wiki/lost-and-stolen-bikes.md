# Lost and stolen bikes

What happens when a bike just... doesn't come back. This covers the automated side of it — what farebox does on its own, before a human ever gets involved.

## Detection

Farebox doesn't wait for anyone to report a bike missing. If a ride has been open for more than 24 hours without the bike being docked anywhere, the ride is closed out automatically and marked as lost. That threshold is generous on purpose — plenty of legitimate rides run long overnight or across a weekend, and we don't want to flag someone who just forgot to check the app.

## Charges

The lost-bike fee doesn't replace the normal ride charge, it stacks on top of it — specifically on top of the capped ride total, not the uncapped one, so a rider isn't double-punished by both an uncapped meter and the fee.

## Lost Fee

Missing the 24-hour docking window right now costs the rider $100.00, charged in addition to whatever the ride itself cost once capped. It shows up as a separate line on the invoice so it's obvious to the rider (and to us, when someone disputes it) that it's not just an unusually expensive ride.

## If a rider disputes it

Check Support playbook first — there's a waiver window support can apply without escalating. If the bike genuinely never turns up, the fee stands; if it was a docking glitch, get dockyard's Sami to check the station logs before refunding anything.
