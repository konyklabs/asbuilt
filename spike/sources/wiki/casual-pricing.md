# Casual rider pricing

Casual riders — anyone without an active membership — pay more per ride than members do, on purpose. This page has the three numbers that make up a casual fare: the unlock fee, the per-minute rate, and the cap that stops any single ride from turning into a surprise bill.

## Unlock

Unlocking a bike without a membership costs a flat $1.00, charged the moment the ride starts regardless of how long it ends up lasting. Think of it as the toll for not having signed up — members skip it entirely, which is one of the arguments we use when pitching the annual plan to someone who rides a few times a week.

## Per Minute

After the unlock fee, a casual ride is billed at $0.25 a minute starting from minute one — there's no free window the way there is for members. A fifteen-minute hop to the train station runs the unlock fee plus fifteen of those per-minute charges, and the meter never pauses just because the bike is stopped at a red light.

## Cap

No matter how long someone forgets to dock the bike, a single ride can't cost more than $30.00 all in. Past that point farebox stops adding minutes to the bill; the ride still has to be closed out normally, but the rider's card only ever sees the capped number. It's saved us more than one angry email from someone who took a bike camping for the weekend.

## Example

A forty-five-minute casual ride: $1.00 to unlock, plus forty-five lots of $0.25 for the minutes, comes to $12.25 — comfortably under the cap.
